Cole Palmer names Chelsea’s ‘best player on the pitch’ in Man Utd win

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →
Cole Palmer says Chelsea fans ‘still don’t know how good Romeo Lavia is’ (Premier League)

Cole Palmer has named Romeo Lavia as ‘the best player on the pitch’ in Chelsea’s 2-0 win over Manchester United on Saturday.

After a goalless first half at Stamford Bridge, Morgan Rogers opened the scoring before Cole Palmer doubled Chelsea’s lead from the penalty spot.

United were also reduced to 10 men as VAR intervened to punish Lisandro Martinez for hitting Wesley Fofana with his forearm.

Lavia, meanwhile, delivered an impressive performance at the heart of Chelsea’s midfield alongside Moises Caicedo.

The 22-year-old was signed in a £53 million deal from Southampton in 2023 but has made just 53 appearances in three years due to multiple injuries, but Palmer believes his Chelsea teammate is one of the Premier League’s most underrated players.

‘Today was one of the best games I’ve seen him [Lavia] play,’ Palmer told BBC Sport after Chelsea’s win over United.

Romeo Lavia impressed at the heart of Chelsea’s midfield in Chelsea’s 2-0 win over Manchester United (AP)

‘I’ve said all along he’s one of my favourite midfielders, people still don’t realise how good he is.

‘Obviously, he’s had his injury problems but he’s trying to put that behind him now and put in performances like today.

‘Today I thought he was the best player on the pitch.’

When asked for his verdict on Lavia’s performance, Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso said: ‘From a midfielder point of view, for me it has been a top, top performance.

‘He’s done so many things right, he’s been always connecting the defence with the attacking players, he’s been able to switch the game, he’s taken many right decisions, he’s been very dynamic, competitive.

‘So I think that not just this game, for the last two, three weeks he’s been playing at a very good level and I’m very happy for him and today he deserves a huge recognition for his performance.’

Palmer, meanwhile, also praised Estevao for his performance on what was his first Premier League start for Chelsea this season.

‘Every time I got the ball I tried to give it to him to show what he can do because I know he’s been wanting to play, he’s been patient and he played well,’ Palmer said.

Xabi Alonso says Estevao deserves more playing time for Chelsea (Getty)

Alonso was also impressed with Estevao’s response after the 19-year-old was left on the bench for Chelsea’s penalty shootout defeat to Liverpool in the Carabao Cup on Wednesday night.

‘We spoke after Tottenham and he knew that he was going to be playing tonight, today,’ Alonso said.

‘He has had the best response to show that he deserves more, that he was prepared and that’s what you want from a football player, so I’m very happy for him.

‘He deserved to enjoy his game, he had a good connection with the crowd. He was really close to scoring that goal. At the end he fought really hard, he was tired and it has been a good day for him.’

Eighth in the Championship might not seem too bad to the casual observer. But for a club of the size of West Ham United, expected to boss the division owing to their finances, their huge following and the massive reputations of most of their players and their manager, this is a crisis.

Anyone in the board room who thinks otherwise needs to go and find another sport to waste their money on.

Six goals conceded on consecutive Saturdays at home in the second division. No passion on the pitch. No drive – save for Jarrod Bowen pulling a goal back.

A static manager who makes no changes at half time after watching that abject first half performance.

Did anybody care, I wondered, other than the unfortunate tens of thousands who had paid hundreds to watch that largely disinterested group of players run around, orchestrated by an incompetent ‘coach’ on the sidelines who seems to be dreaming of a long holiday paid for by the West Ham board when their patience finally, inevitably snaps after watching a side of this value and cost perform like a team of fourth division misfits.

Disjoined substitutions – playing Soucek as a deep sitting, central midfielder when he should have been camped in the opposition ‘box for the last half an hour  – just don’t make sense. Taking off Mukasa again, West Ham’s most gifted midfielder. No plan.

The only ‘plus’ side was that millions of armchair Hammers fans avoided the pain of watching it live. Go watch the highlights and tell me I’m wrong but that manager, with that starting eleven, should have just ordered his own p45. I’ve been solidly behind Nuno and hate the idea of yet more upheaval but – in the immortal words of the Stranglers:

Minute Review: American Airlines 737 MAX 8 First Class

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

American Airlines’ 737 MAX 8 First Class is not going to win awards for innovation, but on a recent flight from Dallas to Burbank, a comfortable seat and a fantastic Texas barbecue dinner made for a very pleasant journey and the subject of this week’s Minute Review.

American Airlines 737 MAX 8 First Class Minute Review (Video)

I’m continuing my series of 60-second flight reviews, and today we’re taking a quick look at American Airlines First Class on the Boeing 737 MAX 8.

There’s nothing particularly revolutionary about the Oasis and seat and many hate it, but at least it’s a standard domestic recliner with more legroom than economy class, power, storage, and individual air vents. For a flight of a few hours, though, it gets the job done just fine. I know some of you think this should not be called “first class” or even “business class” (since it is similar to an international premium economy seat), but AA markets this flight as first class so that’s what I’m calling it.

But the real reason I booked this particular flight was dinner.

I previously wrote about American Airlines’ new Pecan Lodge barbecue platter, which I deliberately routed through Dallas to try. It included smoked beef brisket, sausage, macaroni and cheese, coleslaw, pickles, onions, peppers, and barbecue sauce.

And it was excellent…I’m still salivating over it again today.

The brisket remained tender and juicy despite being reheated onboard, the sausage was flavorful, and even the macaroni and cheese came out wonderfully. This was a genuinely delicious barbecue dinner, not just decent airlines food.
