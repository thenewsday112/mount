United is bringing its nicest plane (with the Polaris Studios) to a city that never sees it

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

United Airlines is bringing its most premium plane to a city where it's rarely spotted.

In January, the Chicago-based carrier will operate a few round trips between Las Vegas and Tokyo, and TPG has learned United plans to use one of its brand-new, luxe Boeing 787-9 Dreamliners on the route.

This is the posh plane United debuted earlier this year, featuring the "Elevated" interior, complete with its next-generation Polaris Suites and the extra-spacious Polaris Studios.

To score a seat on one of those aircraft, you typically have to be flying out of one of United's major hubs like San Francisco or Houston. So far, the carrier has prioritized these planes — which have a whopping 99 premium seats — on routes to destinations where high-end seats are in high demand, like London, Singapore and Seoul.

But it will make a limited appearance on a route United doesn't typically fly: Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) in Las Vegas to Narita International Airport (NRT) in Tokyo.

United Airlines' new Boeing 787-9 "Elevated" Dreamliner. SEAN CUDAHY/THE POINTS GUY

A special route for CES 2027

The occasion for the special route with the luxe plane is the 2027 Consumer Electronics Show, which is set to run in Vegas from Jan. 6 to Jan. 9.

It's one of the biggest conventions in the city each year, which draws visitors from all over the world.

Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) in Las Vegas. SEAN CUDAHY/THE POINTS GUY

Airlines often add special temporary routes around major events — sometimes with planes fit for the occasion, such as when American Airlines sent its popular Airbus "A321T" to Sin City for the Super Bowl in 2024.

We also noted last month how carriers are leaning more heavily into major sporting events, with new and unusual routes focused on college and professional football games.

Caviar, lie-flat beds and a cool snack bar: 17 hours in United's swanky new Polaris suites

Delta also planning special CES routes

United's 787 will certainly be a unique sight at LAS, but top rival Delta Air Lines is the U.S. airline that has historically made the biggest splash at CES.

Nearly four years ago, at that convention, Delta laid out its plans to make on-board Wi-Fi free for all SkyMiles members — a move that nearly every major U.S. carrier matched.

And in January 2025, Delta took over Vegas' iconic Sphere for a keynote presentation by its CEO, Ed Bastian.

Delta Air Lines' January 2025 keynote at the Sphere in Las Vegas. SEAN CUDAHY/THE POINTS GUY

Delta, for its part, is also adding special routes centered around CES.

It'll operate one-off services from Las Vegas to Hong Kong and Taipei, along with extra daily flights during the event to its hubs and its focus city, Austin.

Route Details: United's CES 2027 flights

As for United, its "Elevated" 787 service from LAS to NRT will run on three days toward the tail-end of CES: Jan. 9, 10 and 11.

SEAN CUDAHY/THE POINTS GUY

United's premium-configured 787-9 features:

  • Eight Polaris Studios
  • 56 standard Polaris business-class suites
  • 35 Premium Plus recliners
  • 123 seats in the main cabin, including 33 extra legroom Economy Plus seats

Flights from Tokyo to Las Vegas (Jan. 3, 4 and 5) will be on an older Boeing 777-200ER.

Goth aesthetic gone wild: a user's self-inflicted black screen of non-death in Windows 3.1’s

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Microsoft senior software engineer Raymond Chen has explained what Windows is doing when you hold down the power button, and shared a story about the time a customer accidentally created a Goth Mode for Windows 3.1.

There's a section in The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy (actually, page 116 of this writer's dog-eared copy of The Restaurant at the End of the Universe) in which Zaphod Beeblebrox observes of a ship's interior: "Every time you operate one of these weird black controls that are labeled in black on black background, a little black light lights up black to let you know you've done it."

The same turned out to be true of a user who called Microsoft customer support to complain that after Windows 3.1 booted up, all that remained after the product logo disappeared was a blank screen.

It turned out the customer liked black a little too much and, according to Chen, made themself a Goth theme.

The customer support engineer on Microsoft's end pieced it together. The customer had, according to Chen, "set the wallpaper color to black. They set the title bar color to black. They set the button color to black. They set the window background color to black. They set the window text color to black.

"Every possible color you could pick in the Colors control panel was set to black."

We suspect the mouse pointer, at least, would have stuck around, unless the customer had also managed to change some settings around this. And then there are the icons… However, Windows 3.1 was a long time ago, and this writer's attempts at theming then were the consequences of growing up in the '70s and '80s, which resulted in some interesting neon, orange, and brown combinations.

As for Goth mode, Chen said, "Once you get yourself into this situation, it’s hard to get out because you can't see anything." One solution he suggested was deleting the [colors] section from the win.ini file.

Chen told the anecdote as a coda to the question – what exactly is modern Windows doing when you hold down the power button on a PC? According to Chen, after eight seconds of holding the power button, the Windows kernel realizes a forced power-off is happening and tries to take a kernel dump for diagnostics.

He explained that the kernel dumps were classified by Microsoft as "long power button hold" (LPBH) failures, with one of the bits of information captured indicating if all the pixels on the screen were black. The video driver might therefore be the culprit. Or whatever was responsible for drawing to the screen.

Today's Windows struggles with dark mode. But in the dim, distant past, every pixel being black might mean the user was either Hotblack Desiato or had done something very silly indeed. ®
